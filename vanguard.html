Episode 1: The Iron Meridian

The Detroit River did not freeze quietly that winter; it shuddered under four hundred feet of industrial bedrock. High above the assembly yards, the sky tore open in an unnatural violet scar. Vane-Prime’s orbital siphon had locked its magnetic teeth into the Great Lakes iron meridian, drinking the Earth’s natural frequency to feed the Null Vanguard fleet.

Marcus dug both iron-wrapped boots into the cracked foundry floor. When the anti-matter artillery slammed down, it wasn't just metal meeting fire—it was the weight of a century of soot, foundry slag, and bedrock roaring through his marrow. The impact splintered his gauntlets, sending fissures of bright orange kinetic heat racing up his collarbone.

"Hold your marrow, Marcus!" Mei-Ling’s voice cut sharp through the concussive smoke. She dropped beside him, her fingers striking two slender brass rods. A warm, cyan wave pulsed across his breastplate, arresting the atomic decay and weaving the broken iron back into alignment.

The battle escalates into high orbit.
